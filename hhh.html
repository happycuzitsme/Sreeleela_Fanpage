Pancakes

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 cup milk

Instructions:

  1. Mix dry ingredients.
  2. Add milk and mix until smooth.
  3. Cook on a skillet until golden brown.

Caesar Salad

Ingredients:

  • Romaine lettuce
  • Croutons
  • Caesar dressing

Instructions:

  1. Tear lettuce into pieces.
  2. Add croutons and dressing.
  3. Toss to combine.

Spaghetti Bolognese

Ingredients:

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 100g minced beef
  • Tomato sauce

Instructions:

  1. Cook spaghetti according to package instructions.
  2. Cook beef in a pan until browned.
  3. Add sauce and simmer.
  4. Serve with spaghetti.
